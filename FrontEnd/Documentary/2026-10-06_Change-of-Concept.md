# October 6, 2026 – Change of Concept

**Status:** New project concept adopted

## Summary
We consulted our professor about the gym app idea. Her feedback led us to drop it and choose a new concept.

## What Happened
- The professor explained that the course focuses on **Java and object-oriented programming**, so the front-end would carry less weight in grading.
- Since a gym card and fitness tracker would mostly be UI work, we decided the idea did not fit the course focus.
- We came up with a new concept: a **danger detector for earthquakes and fires**.

## New Concept
A Java-based "danger scanner":
- A camera is installed to scan the surroundings for danger.
- Each detected hazard comes with a **description**.
- Each hazard is assigned a **danger level**.
- The system gives **recommendations** on what to do.

## Decision
Replace the gym app with the danger detector. The team moved on to preparing the project proposal.

Whole team (Jacob, Dan, Martin)
